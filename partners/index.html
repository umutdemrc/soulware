How it works

From idea to App Store in four steps.

We test every idea with your audience before anyone writes code, so we only build what your audience asks for.

1

A 20-minute call

We learn about your audience and what you already offer. We bring three app ideas, and you bring yours.

2

Test it with your audience

You post a short poll and a waitlist link. We prepare every image and caption. Your audience decides.

3

We build it

If the test passes, we sign one short agreement and build the first version by a date we set together.

4

Launch and grow

You share it in your own style. We run everything else: the app, subscriptions, updates and support.

The deal

Simple, written down, and fair both ways.

50 / 50

We split the app's net profit equally: what Apple pays us, minus only the app's own running costs (such as AI and servers). Our salaries and development time are never deducted.

No cost to you

You never pay us, not even in a slow month. Building the app is our investment.

You see the numbers

You get read-only access to the revenue dashboard and a statement every month.

You keep earning

If you ever stop promoting, you keep your share on the subscribers you brought, for as long as they stay.

Your name stays yours

You own your name, image, voice and method. We use them in the app only while we work together.

Fair on growth

Everyone who joins is shared, except people we bring in ourselves through our own tracked ads.

One short agreement

A few pages in plain language, plus a one-page plan we fill in together. No lock-in period.

FAQ

Good questions to ask.

What kind of creators do you work with?

Creators who teach something people practise: fitness, yoga, languages, music, art, study, habits, pets and more. Most have between 30K and 500K followers. We don't build finance, medical, gambling or fortune-telling apps.

Who owns the app?

Soulware owns the code and the App Store listing. You own your name, image, voice and content, and you license them to the app while we work together.

How much do I have to post?

We agree it together before launch, in formats and at a pace that fit your style, and we review it every three months. Promotion is the heart of the partnership, so it has to work for you.

What if my audience isn't interested?

Then we don't build it, and neither of us owes anything. We can try another idea.

Do I have to create new content?

Only if the app needs your material, and only what we agree in the plan. Many apps can be built from what you already teach.

What happens if we stop working together?

There's no fixed term. If it ends, you keep your 50% on the subscribers you brought for as long as they stay subscribed, and we remove your name and image from the app.

Have an audience that would love an app?

Tell me a little about what you teach. I reply to every message personally.
